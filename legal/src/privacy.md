# Checkshot Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective November 16, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Ibrahim Shaheed, operating as Checkshot ("we"), collects through the Checkshot software and the checkshot.ca website, and what we do with it. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act where they apply.

## The short version

- The Checkshot app works offline. It does not send your photos, point clouds, survey data, files, usage or crash reports to us. There is no account, no tracking and no analytics in the app.
- The website has no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.
- If you email us or buy a licence, we use what you give us only to answer you and to provide your licence.

## The app

**Your files stay on your computer.** Everything you load into Checkshot is processed on your own computer.

**Settings** (such as your coordinate system choice, a detected survey-grid shift, the beta/licence status and the latest date the app has seen, used to check the beta period) are stored only on your computer.

**Optional satellite map.** If you turn on the satellite map and enter your own ArcGIS key, the app requests map tiles for the area on screen directly from Esri. Esri receives that request under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive nothing.

**Optional place search** sends the text you search for to Esri's geocoding service in the same way, only when you use it.

**Date check.** If an app version uses an online date check to detect a computer clock that has been set back, it makes a standard web request to a public website to read the current date. It sends no information about you or your files.

## The website

The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit; see GitHub's privacy statement. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on the site, and we do not receive those logs.

## Email and licences

If you email support@checkshot.ca, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply and keep a record of support. If you buy a licence, we keep your name, email, the licence details and purchase records, and our payment provider processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; we do not see or store full card numbers. We keep purchase records as long as tax and accounting law requires, and support emails for up to three years.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with service providers we need to run the business (such as our email and payment providers), or when the law requires it. Some providers may store information outside Canada, where it is subject to the laws of that country.

## Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (unless we must keep it by law) by emailing support@checkshot.ca.

## Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
