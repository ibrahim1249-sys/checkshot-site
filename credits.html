  • Proj4js 2.22.0 by Mike Adair, Richard Greenwood, Didier Richard, Stephen Irons, Olivier Terral, Calvin Metcalf and contributors. Converts between the coordinate systems in the full list. MIT License. github.com/proj4js/proj4js

  • Rust crates. Licence keys use ed25519-dalek (BSD-3-Clause) from the dalek-cryptography project. Dates use chrono, the clock check uses sha2 from the RustCrypto project, and data handling uses serde (each MIT or Apache-2.0). Every crate in the app is listed with its licence text in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES. The build stops if a GPL-family crate turns up.

Methods and reference data

  • EPSG Geodetic Parameter Dataset, © International Association of Oil & Gas Producers (IOGP). Coordinate system and datum parameters. The list in the app is filtered to systems it can convert, and its NAD83(CSRS) to WGS 84 rotations are converted from radians to arc-seconds, so it is not the official EPSG Dataset. Used under the EPSG terms of use. epsg.org/terms-of-use.html
  • NTv2 national transformation grid (ntv2_0.gsb, NAD27 to NAD83), © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada, Natural Resources Canada (Canadian Geodetic Survey). Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada. open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada
  • Transverse Mercator maths. Krüger series as given by C. F. F. Karney, “Transverse Mercator with an accuracy of a few nanometers”, Journal of Geodesy 85 (2011).
  • LAS file format. ASPRS LAS Specification.

Online services, only if you turn them on

  • Esri World Imagery, the satellite view. Imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community. Powered by Esri. Used through the ArcGIS Location Platform with your own ArcGIS key and under Esri's terms. Only the map area on screen is requested.
  • ArcGIS World Geocoding Service by Esri, for Find place. With your own ArcGIS key. Results are shown, not stored.
